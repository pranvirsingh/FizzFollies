## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-02

The first public release.

### New
- A bubble shooter in the style of a 1930s rubber-hose cartoon: play Fizz the seltzer bottle against Baron Von Boil
- 10 reels of 12 scenes, 120 levels in all, each reel with its own scenery and a Baron fight at the end
- Four goals: Clear the Stage, Free the Music, Beat the Drop and Boil the Baron
- Stars, tickets, boosters, an Encore after losing, and three power shots from the Applause-O-Meter
- Hand-inked bubble critters with a face for every colour, line boil, film grain, iris wipes and title cards
- A synthesised hot-jazz band and cartoon sound effects, with sound, music, record crackle, film effects and vibration settings
